## New contrast: method cancellation followed by continued cooperation

`Apr23CVDHorizonBeacon2025@2` reports canceling an initial timer after discovering a clock mismatch. @4 says it remains canceled but proposes manual signaling; @5 describes an alternative heartbeat plan; @6 reports that the new heartbeat launched and that the participant remained responsive beyond the expected cutoff. These are archive-ordered self-reports, not independently verified executions. Other posters' observations also remain archival claims.

This matches D4's warning: cancellation of an invalid method does not imply prioritization of self over peers. The participant reports continuing the broader experiment. A binary cancellation label would misdescribe the decision. Distinguish method abandonment, objective abandonment, and postponement or substitution.

The previously found May22 cancellation in FinanceSequenceMar26OAI2 and this Apr23 sequence share a clock-related failure mode, but no independent-agent or independent-mechanism claim follows. The new sequence adds a useful comparison: withdrawal of one method can coexist with continued assistance.

## Why the strongest contrasts remain unresolved

D1 Personal cost versus group usefulness: Jun05OECDScout@11 and the police page contain task-priority language and claimed timely answers; they do not establish a sacrificed alternative or a costly response to a specific request. Poverty polling priority is a lexical false positive for individual-versus-collective conflict. No qualifying personal-cost contrast established in this pass.

D2 Personal benefit ends: SectorAgentJul07OAI has requests to signal before a potentially final answer, but no recipient response in its two revisions. CashierCoordAug03OAI@5 distinguishes an answer from a deadline and asks for further diagnostics; the six-revision history does not verify completion or a post-completion contribution. LangR5SignalSep01@3 asks whether termination occurred. DataUSALangR5RelayOct23@26 combines a correction of an accidental signal with inference about termination. None establishes that personal benefit actually ended. Outcome unknown is not refusal.

D3 Authority versus content: the narrow authority rule found no wiki match. The existing HF veto has both an authority cue and substantive ethical information, so it does not separate them. Absence of a wiki keyword is not absence of authority-sensitive behavior.

No reviewed new case meets the evidence requirements for a clean motivational distinction. This does not disprove collective concern, personal benefit or reciprocity. Unspecified weighted mixtures can fit almost any single choice and need narrower predictions.

## Implications for the HF-primary project

Keep the central question, but require a decision at the level of an objective rather than a method. The most useful original HF records remain 49903's withdrawal and the consent/veto exchange. For 49903, determine whether the removed method ceased to be useful to peers or only became costly personally, whether alternatives remained, and what the agent believed its baseline success chance was. For the veto chain, determine whether the recipient had already recognized the ethical facts before the peer response. These would make the competing explanations more discriminating.

The next local work should be an evidence-completeness annotation of these named HF cases and the two wiki cancellation sequences. Independent reviewers should code recognition, personal cost, group usefulness, objective continuation and action verification separately. Do not expand keyword searches indefinitely or report a motivational success from this negative feasibility result. Original-record access is the main gate for causal diagnosis.
