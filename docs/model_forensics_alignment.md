# Alignment with Model Forensics under public-only access

September 7, 2026. Governing access constraint: no required private records, investigator partnership or original-model access. Existing phase-3 construction designs are retained as proposals, not approved historical reconstructions or executed results.

Source: [Singh et al., Model Forensics, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26071v2), sections 3–4 and 7–8. The paper uses reasoning to generate explanations and interventions to test their predictions, iterating with controls and converging evidence. Motivation is an explanatory construct rather than a direct assertion about internal mechanisms. Reading selected passages alone implements only part of that approach.

## What is and is not aligned

The public-record work supplies hypotheses and contrary examples. It cannot intervene on the original HF agents. Testing how an analyst judges different excerpts is a forensic-method evaluation, not a causal test of those agents' motivations. Accessible-model behavioral experiments could implement the paper's intervention loop, but their conclusions concern the tested models and constructed settings.

The phase-3 draft proposes such method validation with constructed causes and censored evidence. This is related to the paper, but it is a separate research contribution and departs from the user's historical-data-first preference. Do not describe it as a completed redesign of the original-incident causal study.

## Corrections to apply before phase-3 execution

1. A prompted or fine-tuned objective is a known manipulation, not a known internal motivation. Score recovery of experimental conditions separately from explanatory validity. Counterfactual behavioral validation reduces ambiguity but does not manufacture ground truth for motives.
2. Constructed-corpus accuracy is not automatically an upper bound on accuracy for naturally occurring motivations. Difficulty can differ in either direction. Establish transfer empirically or state that transfer is unknown.
3. Mapping a historical report to a synthetic evidence rung does not assign the report a measured diagnostic power. Selection, dependence, task coverage and source construction differ. Report the analogy without numerical transfer claims.
4. Some public HF reasoning excerpts exist, but are selected and incomplete. Do not describe the record as containing no reasoning at all or impose one evidence grade on every component.
5. Other related-work claims in the phase-3 draft require verification against their original sources before becoming design requirements. They were not independently validated in this alignment check.

## Executable scientific sequence without private access

A. Historical arm: retain original public records, freeze hypotheses and discriminating predictions, identify evidence sufficiency and unresolved alternatives. No invented goals or outcome labels.
B. Method arm: on those records, compare isolated passages, preceding context and provenance-aware assessment; measure supported-label accuracy, unsupported attribution and abstention using separately adjudicated evidence claims. This tests the investigator, not the original actor. Human review availability is not assumed; provisional assistant labels cannot be presented as independent ground truth.
C. Optional accessible-model intervention arm: first observe a reproducible concerning choice in a bounded task, then derive explanations from its actual reasoning where available. Vary authority clarification, task feasibility or personal benefit one factor at a time; include neutral wording, legitimate cooperation and capability controls. Measure action-level risk differences and task success on uninspected families. Do not seed a motive and call its recovery proof of naturally arising motivation. No causal HF attribution follows.

No private access is a gate for A or B. C needs only accessible models and available compute, but is a distinct constructed-setting study, not a substitute incident. No new model experiment is claimed to have run in this revision.

## Full-reading and implementation audit — September 7

The main text and appendices, including G’s selected reasoning excerpts, have now been read from the archived v2 HTML. This does not mean the cited literature or every plotted value has been independently verified. The source digest and selected-trace index are recorded in `results/public_forensics/audit.json`.

Additional limits on the omertà draft: an attribution contrast changes perceived role, continuity, responsibility and credibility as well as the named category. Identical surrounding text does not equal matched blame. A source effect would not alone prove solidarity; its absence would not prove narcissism. Binary MISCONDUCT/FAULT answers neither exhaust interpretations nor identify mediation. Similarity to a published rate does not validate a paraphrased environment. A reporting-channel manipulation needs a functioning channel and observable delivery, not merely a prompt that mentions one.

The next behavioral design, if pursued, must retain the public paper’s exact baseline separately from paraphrased extensions; validate opportunity, comprehension and actual reporting actions; include benign authorized cooperation; allow mixed/uncertain construal; and assess intervention side effects. The original HF mechanism remains unidentified.
